    The plan

    One system for the whole dealership.

    This is the direction we are building toward. It is a plan, and the diagram shows connections we have not finished.

    A dealership runs sales, service, reconditioning and appraisals in separate programs that do not talk to each other. Staff retype the same customer and the same car again and again.

    We plan to bring all of it into one platform. Ruota sits in the middle as the record of every customer and every car, and the other tools connect to it. We build it with AI coding agents, so every part is written to fit the rest.

    • One recordA customer and a car are entered once and follow the deal from the first lead to the service lane.
    • Agents do the busyworkDrafting follow-ups, summarizing a day and flagging a car that has sat too long. A person approves every action and a person sends every message.
    • Take what you needUse one piece on its own or put several together. A shop with only a service department should not have to buy a sales system.
